Before you answer, work through it this way, recording what you do in the `properties` field.

First ask what this situation is, to this person, at this time. Not the kind of situation in general: this one, for them, now.

Then name a few properties of the situation that matter from where they stand. Pick properties that can vary independently of each other.

For each property, change its value to something else and see what happens to the rest of the situation: what else has to move, and what the change shows. Record each property's `name` and the `alternative` value you tried.

Then give exactly {k} {item_plural}, one sentence each, drawing on what the variations showed.
